# Permanent project charter

Authority: `MASTER_REQUIREMENTS.md`, the complete preserved user brief. All detailed requirements there remain in force even when summarized here.

Build a cross-industry autonomous scientific, technical, creative, consumer and commercial discovery lab: explore problems; retrieve prior knowledge and limitations; identify contradictions and cross-domain connections; generate falsifiable original hypotheses; design controlled experiments; execute only authorized isolated tests; independently evaluate; replicate and investigate failures; preserve evidence and research memory; guide subsequent research; develop candidate applications; investigate real markets; distinguish external demand from speculation; operate within limits; recover from interruptions and exhausted resources.

The long-term objective is sustained evidence-guided discovery across industries. No industry receives exclusive eligibility. The immediate milestone is a reproducible minimum research loop, followed by the remaining phase gates in dependency order.

Three separate success standards: technical means a measurable experimental result; discovery means a meaningful insight with novelty assessed against prior work; commercial means external evidence of a real problem, identifiable economic buyer, and credible ability or willingness to pay. No standard implies another. An empty search is not novelty evidence. Synthetic interviews never validate a market.

Permanent principles: persistent typed state; deterministic gates; append-only provenance and content hashes with independently retained anchors; independent evaluation; reproducibility; provider independence; least privilege; bounded spending, time, concurrency and retries; cancellation and recovery; honest uncertainty. Retrieved text is untrusted data. Models propose, deterministic policy decides. Generated code never runs in the orchestrator.

Scope includes all phases 0–9 in the master brief. CLI first, modular monolith, Python 3.12/uv/Pydantic/SQLite WAL. Optional distributed/physical infrastructure is deferred until an evidenced need and authorization exist. Non-goals: generic chatbot, startup idea generator, claims of automated scientific proof, arbitrary code execution without a real sandbox, fabricated evidence, automatic deployment/merge, or spending without authorization. Direction changes require a decision record tied to evidence, an impact audit of stable requirements, and user authorization for material scope changes. A deadline never waives verification; hackathon eligibility is unconfirmed.
